Privacy Policy

Sticker.pi Testnet uses Pi SDK authentication and processes only the information needed to operate the app, such as your Pi user ID and username.

Future game progress, album state, packs, trades and achievements will be stored only when those features are enabled. No wallet seed phrase or private key is requested or stored by the app.

This Testnet build is for development and testing. Data structures and features may change before Mainnet release.
