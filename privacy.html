Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Sticker.pi Testnet uses Pi SDK authentication and processes only the information needed to operate the test app, including the Pi user ID, username and public wallet address returned after user authorization. Public wallet-address consent supports the Testnet A2U test; distinct recipient metrics store a server-side hash of the verified address.

Authoritative gameplay data may include level, XP, daily streak, sticker collection, pack inventory, duplicates, challenge statistics, limited anti-abuse timestamps and verified Testnet transaction identifiers. A non-authoritative display cache may also be stored on the user's device.

The Daily Top 10 stores a Pioneer’s best verified result for the UTC day and publicly displays the authorized Pi username, score, accuracy and best combo. Testnet ranking records are temporary, remain separate from Mainnet and may be reset during development.

Sticker.pi never asks users for, stores or needs a Pi Wallet passphrase, private key or seed phrase.

Supported Testnet transactions use Pi Network's official SDK and APIs and are verified server-side. Vercel and Upstash may process minimal technical data required to host the test service and persistent database.

This build is for development and testing. Test-Pi has no Mainnet value, and Testnet gameplay data may be reset as the service evolves.

Peer-to-peer exchange is not active. The controlled A2U developer test is available after authentication and limited to five distinct recipient wallets.
