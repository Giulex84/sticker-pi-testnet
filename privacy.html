Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Sticker.pi Testnet uses Pi SDK authentication and processes only information needed to operate the app, including your Pi user ID and username.

Authoritative gameplay data is stored server-side and may include level, XP, daily streak, sticker collection, pack inventory, duplicates, challenge statistics, limited anti-abuse timestamps and verified Testnet purchase identifiers. A non-authoritative display cache may also be stored on your device.

Sticker.pi never asks for, stores or needs your Pi Wallet passphrase, private key or seed phrase.

Payment processing uses Pi Network's official SDK and APIs. Supported Testnet transactions are verified server-side before a pack is granted. Service providers such as Vercel and Upstash may process minimal technical request data required to host the app and database.

This Testnet build is for development and testing. Test data may be reset or migrated as features evolve before any Mainnet release.
