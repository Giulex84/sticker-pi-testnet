Privacy Policy

Sticker.pi Testnet uses Pi SDK authentication and processes only information needed to operate the app, including your Pi user ID and username.

Gameplay data may include level, XP, daily streak, sticker collection, pack inventory, duplicates, challenge statistics and verified Testnet purchase identifiers. During early Testnet development this data may be stored on your device; when cloud persistence is enabled it may also be stored in the app's server-side database.

Sticker.pi never asks for, stores or needs your Pi Wallet passphrase, private key or seed phrase.

Payment processing uses Pi Network's official SDK and APIs. The app verifies supported Testnet transactions server-side before treating them as completed.

This Testnet build is for development and testing. Test data may be reset or migrated as features evolve before any Mainnet release.
